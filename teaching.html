Department of Computer Science, Khalifa University, Abu Dhabi, UAE

Served as teaching assistant, lab instructor, and project mentor for four undergraduate computing courses over five years, accumulating ten semesters of experience across lecturing and lab instruction. Each lab session opened with a short lecture on the topic before transitioning to the hands-on exercise.

Comprehensive survey of data science and AI with engineering applications. Topics include data analysis and visualisation, machine learning, deep learning, computer vision, natural language processing, autonomous driving, and AI ethics. Students gain hands-on experience with Python-based AI and data science libraries.

5 semesters

ENGR 114 — Introduction to Computing: Python

Foundations of programming and computational problem-solving using Python. Topics include data types, control flow, functions, data structures (lists, tuples, databases), and file I/O.

2 semesters

ENGR 113 — Introduction to Computing: MATLAB

Programming principles and computational workflows using MATLAB. Topics include arrays, functions, scripts, logical operators, and introductory data analysis and input/output operations.

2 semesters

ENGR 112 — Introduction to Computing: C++

Introduction to structured programming using C++. Topics include data types, conditional and repetitive structures, functions, arrays, strings, and file handling.

Outreach · Dec. 15–19, 2025

Instructor, AI Winter Camp 2025

Khalifa University (Outreach Department) in partnership with the Abu Dhabi Department of Education and Knowledge (ADEK) · Theme: “AI for a Sustainable Future” · 63 high-school students, Grades 10–12, selected through an academic interview process. Delivered lectures and supervised hands-on sessions throughout the five-day program.

Day 1

ML fundamentals, Python environment setup, dataset import, model training and evaluation, OpenCV image capture.

Day 2

Data quality and its impact on ML model performance; hands-on data cleaning exercises.

Day 3

Comparative study of ML models; introduction of the Grand Challenge dataset.

Day 4

Grand Challenge completion — students trained models and integrated them into applications; judging and evaluation.

Day 5

AI ethics; winner announcement; certificate distribution.
